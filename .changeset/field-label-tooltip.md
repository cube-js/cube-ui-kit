**Breaking:** the info badge next to a field's label is now `labelTooltip`, not `tooltip`. This applies to every field component (`TextInput`, `TextArea`, `CommandTextArea`, `NumberInput`, `PasswordInput`, `SearchInput`, `ColorInput`, `ComboBox`, `SearchComboBox`, `Select`, `Picker`, `FilterPicker`, `ListBox`, `FilterListBox`, `Checkbox`, `CheckboxGroup`, `Switch`, `RadioGroup`, `Slider` and its variants, the date and time pickers, `FileInput`, `ColorPicker`, `ColorSwatchGroup`, `TextInputMapper`), to the legacy `Field` / `Form.Item`, and to `FieldWrapper`. `tooltip` now means only the control's own tooltip, as it does on `Item` and `Button`. Previously a component that is both a field and a trigger had one prop meaning two things: a labelled `Select` rendered its `tooltip` on the trigger and again as a label badge, and `tooltip={true}` on a labelled `Picker` or `FilterPicker` rendered an empty badge.

Migrating: rename `tooltip` to `labelTooltip` wherever it described the label, whether on the field or on a legacy `Field` / `Form.Item` wrapping it. TypeScript flags the old spelling on every field except `Select` and `Checkbox`, where `tooltip` is valid as the control's own: the `Select` trigger's tooltip, and `Checkbox`'s new hover tooltip over the box and its inline label. Search for `Select` and `Checkbox` elements with both `label` and `tooltip` by hand and move the prop to `labelTooltip` if the label badge is what it was for (on `Select` it previously rendered in both places). JS callers, `as any` and untyped spreads are not flagged either; in development, the first labelled field — other than `Select` and `Checkbox` — that still receives `tooltip` logs a one-time console warning naming `labelTooltip`. The trigger tooltips of `Picker`, `FilterPicker` and `ColorPicker` remain `triggerTooltip`.
