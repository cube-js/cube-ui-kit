`ComboBox`, `SearchComboBox` and `CommandTextArea`: while the list is open, the first click on an enabled control outside it now acts on that control and closes the list, as a click on a UI Kit `Button` already did. A control is a checkbox, switch, radio, text field, native button, link or any other focusable element, in the dialog, popover or menu the field sits in, or anywhere on the page when the field is in none of them. Such a click used to only close the list, so a checkbox stayed unchanged and a text field took no focus until a second click. Focus moves to the control, so the field settles its typed text first, as it does on `Tab`. A click anywhere else still only closes the list and leaves the field focused with its text: on blank space, a dialog's surface or backdrop, or a control outside the dialog or popover the field sits in. A click on a disabled control, including a disabled UI Kit `Button`, which used to commit the typed text, keeps the field's focus and text as well. An enabled UI Kit `Button` or `ItemButton` is still reached from anywhere. `TagInput` now treats blank space in a dialog and controls outside the dialog or popover it sits in the same way; a click there used to move focus away and add the typed text as a tag.
