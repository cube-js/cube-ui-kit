`ComboBox`, `SearchComboBox` and `CommandTextArea`: while the list is open, the first click on an enabled control outside it now acts on that control and closes the list, as a click on a UI Kit `Button` already did. A control is a checkbox, switch, radio, text field, native button, link or any other focusable element, in the same dialog as the field, or anywhere on the page when the field is not in a dialog. Such a click used to only close the list, so a checkbox stayed unchanged and a text field took no focus until a second click. Focus moves to the control, so the field settles its typed text first, as it does on `Tab`. A click anywhere else still only closes the list and leaves the field focused with its text: on blank space, a dialog's surface or backdrop, or a control outside the popover or dialog the field sits in. A click on a disabled control changes nothing. `TagInput` now treats blank space in a dialog and controls outside its popover the same way; a click there used to move focus away and add the typed text as a tag.
