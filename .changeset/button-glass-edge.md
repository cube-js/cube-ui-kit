Outline and primary buttons now draw their edge as a glass rim (a light highlight along the top and bottom, a soft shade along the sides) instead of a solid border, and sit on a small drop shadow that collapses while pressed. Sizes and layout are unchanged. The rim comes from the new `$glass-shadow` (neutral surfaces) and `$glass-accent-shadow` (brand fills) tokens, which adapt to dark and high-contrast schemes.
