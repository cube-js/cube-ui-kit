`Layout.Panel` and `Layout.Pane` share a new resize handle. At rest the resizable edge is a border-width line (a panel's own border, or a line the pane now draws), with no gray track and no dots; on hover, while dragging and on keyboard focus it turns into an accent line one pixel wider (2px, or 1.5px over a .5px hairline), centred on a panel's border, and keyboard focus adds a focus ring. At the minimum or maximum size the cursor shows the one direction left. On touch screens a grip shows on the edge and a coarse pointer gets a 20px target. The handle now exposes `aria-valuenow`, `aria-valuemin` and `aria-valuemax` (a pane without a `maxSize` uses the room its container has) and `aria-controls`; `Home` / `End` resize to the minimum / maximum. Panel and pane sizes stay as they were. The content beside a resizable panel loses the 2px gap the old track filled and sits against the panel's border, as beside a fixed panel, so it is 2px wider. Breaking for custom styles: `Layout.Pane` no longer renders the `Drag` and `DragPart` sub-elements; its `ResizeHandler` now holds `Line`, `Grip` and `TouchArea`, which the pane's `styles` reach. A pane that drew its own border on the resizable edge to show it can drop that border.
