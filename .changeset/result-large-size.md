`Result` gains what a confirmation or result dialog needs: an `actions` slot for `Result.Action` buttons, which take their size from the Result, a `size="large"` scale with a bigger icon and text, and a `layout="stacked"` arrangement that fills the container and stacks the actions to the full width. Size and layout are independent, so a large result can keep its centered row of actions and a medium one can stack them. `isCompact` keeps its own scale and arrangement and ignores both. The status icon now has room around it that grows with its size, a quarter of the icon on each side, in every non-compact Result including the default medium one, a custom `icon` gets the same room (sized by the Result's `--icon-size`, so size a custom icon through it rather than on the icon), and that room is the whole distance to the title, so the title sits closer to the icon than before. Outside `isCompact` the content and the actions now keep their `3x` with a top margin instead of the container `gap`, so a `gap` passed to such a Result adds to it. A status without an icon (`404`, `403`, `500`) renders no icon slot. The stacked layout keeps a `1.5x` padding of its own on top of the host's.
