`ListBox` takes `listGap`, the space between options in pixels. It defaults to the hairline options had before. It spaces flat, virtualized, sectioned and reorderable lists alike: a flat list is virtualized, so it ignored a margin set through `optionStyles`. `FilterListBox`, `Picker`, `FilterPicker`, `ComboBox`, `SearchComboBox`, `TagInput`, `CommandTextArea` and `Select` pass it to their list.
