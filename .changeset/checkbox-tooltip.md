Add `tooltip` to `Checkbox`: a tooltip shown on hover over the box and its inline label, standalone or inside a `Checkbox.Group`, and also while the checkbox is disabled — a place to say why an option is unavailable. It opens on keyboard focus too, and describes the input to screen readers. It takes a string or `TooltipProvider` props. It is separate from `labelTooltip`, the info badge next to a field label.
